# 03. The Supermarket Synergistic Protocol: Food-Derived Bioactives in Plaque Stabilization

## Overview
While single dietary agents fail to reverse established plaques, a synergistic formulation combining five biochemically complementary whole-food sources targets all major hallmarks of atherogenesis.

---

## The 5 Pillars & Molecular Validation

### 1. Oat Bran (Soluble Beta-Glucan)
- **Dose:** 40–50 g daily (3–4 tablespoons) providing ~3–5 g soluble $\beta$-glucan.
- **Mechanism:** Viscous gel formation sequesters intestinal bile acids, disrupting enterohepatic circulation. The liver upregulates $CYP7A1$ and $LDLR$, lowering circulating LDL-C by 10–12%.
- **Colonic Fermentation:** Produces propionate, which allosterically inhibits hepatic HMG-CoA reductase.

### 2. Aged / Fermented Black Garlic (S-Allylcysteine)
- **Molecular Docking:** S-Allylcysteine binds human eNOS (PDB 1M9Q) with **$\Delta G = -5.42\ \text{kcal/mol}$** ($K_d = 151.4\ \mu\text{M}$).
- **Clinical Evidence:** In randomized double-blind trials conducted by UCLA Cardiology (Dr. Matthew Budoff et al.), Aged Garlic Extract reduced **Low-Attenuation Plaque (LAP)** volume by 1.5–3.0% per year.
- **Fresh Garlic Rule:** If using fresh garlic, crush and let rest for **10 minutes** before consumption to allow alliinase to convert alliin to allicin.

### 3. Pure 100% Pomegranate Juice (Urolithin A)
- **Molecular Docking:** The gut-derived metabolite Urolithin A binds human **Paraoxonase-1 (PON1, PDB 1V04)** with high affinity: **$\Delta G = -7.82\ \text{kcal/mol}$** ($K_d = 3.08\ \mu\text{M}$).
- **Clinical Evidence:** Aviram et al. (*Clin Nutr* 2004) demonstrated up to **30% reduction in carotid intima-media thickness (cIMT)** after 1 year in patients with severe carotid stenosis.
- **Dose:** 100–150 mL daily of 100% unsweetened direct juice.

### 4. Extra Virgin Cold-Pressed Olive Oil (Hydroxytyrosol & Oleocanthal)
- **Molecular Docking:** Hydroxytyrosol binds eNOS with **$\Delta G = -6.29\ \text{kcal/mol}$** ($K_d = 36.8\ \mu\text{M}$).
- **Mechanism:** Scavenges peroxyl radicals in LDL particles, preventing oxLDL recognition by macrophage scavenger receptor CD36.
- **Clinical Evidence:** PREDIMED trial (*NEJM*) demonstrated a 30% reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE).
- **Dose:** 25–40 mL (2–3 tablespoons) daily, consumed raw/uncooked.

### 5. Natto / Fermented Soy (Nattokinase & Vitamin K2 / MK-7)
- **Nattokinase (3000–6000 FU):** Cleaves microthrombi and smooths endothelial shear stress (*Front Cardiovasc Med* 2022, 36.6% plaque reduction in 1,062 patients).
- **Vitamin K2 (Menaquinone-7, ~900 µg/100g):** Essential cofactor for $\gamma$-glutamyl carboxylase to carboxylate **Matrix Gla Protein (MGP)**, the body's primary endogenous calcification inhibitor.
- **Dose:** 40–50 g (1 pack) 2–3 times per week. (Contraindicated with Vitamin K antagonists like Warfarin).
